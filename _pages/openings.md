<section class="join-intro">
  <h1>Join Our Team</h1>
  <p>We are always looking for talented individuals who are passionate about using computational and statistical approaches to improve our understanding of human health and disease. Please do not hesitate to <a href="mailto:zyu5@mgh.harvard.edu?subject=Interest%20in%20joining%20the%20Zhi%20Yu%20Lab">contact us</a>. Below are examples of fellowships that may support trainees, followed by previous opportunities in the lab.</p>
</section>

<section class="join-fellowships" aria-labelledby="fellowships">
  <h2 id="fellowships">Fellowships</h2>

  <div class="join-fellowship-list">
    <article>
      <h3><a href="https://grants.nih.gov/grants/guide/notice-files/NOT-HL-24-026.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">NHLBI TOPMed Fellowship</a></h3>
      <p>The TOPMed Fellowship supports early-career researchers applying AI and machine learning approaches to data-driven questions in heart, lung, blood, and sleep research, with opportunities to work with TOPMed’s rich trans-omics resources.</p>
    </article>

    <article>
      <h3><a href="https://professional.heart.org/en/research-programs/aha-funding-opportunities/postdoctoral-fellowship" target="_blank" rel="noopener">American Heart Association Postdoctoral Fellowship</a></h3>
      <p>The American Heart Association offers one- or two-year fellowships for postdoctoral researchers working in cardiovascular and brain health research.</p>
    </article>

    <article>
      <h3><a href="https://baef.be/research-fellowships-be/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Belgian American Educational Foundation Research Fellowships</a></h3>
      <p>BAEF fellowships support eligible Belgian researchers—including master’s-level, doctoral, and postdoctoral trainees—pursuing research in the United States.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
